# From Seed to Smoke
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Film preview, captions and screening page**

### Goal and audience

Introduce Episode 1 through actual cultivation footage and an accessible short preview. The immediate audience is documentary viewers and screening hosts.

### Design and implementation

The edition's captioned field film links the public series to the grow room. Preview media and subtitles are versioned together. Time-coded captions travel with the preview. A screening page should identify the film, episode and time without implying the entire episode has been uploaded.

### What a contributor can establish

The public entry page returned HTTP 200 and rendered local content in Chromium at a 390 × 844 viewport. A preview playback is distinct from premiere attendance. A later screening needs a consenting host, a licensed master and actual attendance evidence.

### Evidence and outstanding work

The included film is a preview. The complete episode, premiere attendance and ad placement remain unverified. The next acceptance case is concrete. Play the preview locally with captions and identify the Episode 1 premiere time.

### Place in POTIFY

Qualify one screening-host or episode-sponsor conversation after confirming media rights and delivery. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
